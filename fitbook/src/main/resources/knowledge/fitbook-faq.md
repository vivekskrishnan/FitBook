# FitBook Knowledge Base

## About FitBook
FitBook is an online appointment scheduling system for a fitness-training studio. Customers browse open
time slots, book a session with a trainer, and can cancel their own bookings. Trainers publish their own
availability slots. Every booking and cancellation sends a confirmation message to the customer.

## Studio hours
The studio is open Monday to Friday from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Saturday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Sunday
from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Sessions can only be booked inside a trainer's published availability slots, so
the times you see on the Slots page are the only times you can book.

## Services and pricing
FitBook offers two services. Weight training is a 60-minute weight room session with a personal trainer and
costs $45.00 per session. Pilates is a 45-minute Pilates class and costs $20.00 per session. Prices are per
session and are charged at the studio at the time of the visit; there is no online payment.

## How to book
Log in as a customer, open the Slots page, choose an open slot and confirm the booking. A slot can be booked
by only one customer. If two people try to book the same slot at the same moment, the first one wins and the
second is told the slot is no longer available. You can also ask the AI assistant to find a slot and book it
for you; the assistant always shows you a summary first and only books after you say yes.

## Cancellation policy
Customers can cancel their own upcoming appointments from the My Appointments page, or by asking the
assistant. You can only cancel your own appointments. Please cancel at least 24 hours before the session so
the trainer can plan their day. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session may be recorded as a
late cancellation. Once an appointment is cancelled, that slot is not reopened for rebooking; the customer
should choose a different open slot instead.

## Rescheduling
FitBook does not have a reschedule button. To change your time, cancel the existing appointment and book a
different open slot.

## Late arrivals and no-shows
Arrive 5 minutes early. Sessions start and end at the booked time, so a late arrival does not extend the
session. If you do not show up and have not cancelled, the appointment counts as a no-show.

## Notifications and reminders
You receive a confirmation message when you book and when you cancel. FitBook also sends an automated,
personalized reminder before an upcoming appointment. If the notification service is temporarily
unavailable, your booking or cancellation still goes through; only the message may be delayed or missed.

## What to bring
Bring a water bottle, a towel and comfortable workout clothing. Weight training sessions require closed-toe
athletic shoes. Pilates sessions are done in socks or barefoot and mats are provided by the studio.

## Accounts and privacy
Customers and trainers have separate roles. Customers can only see and cancel their own appointments, and
trainers can only manage their own availability. Passwords are stored hashed and are never shown to anyone,
including the assistant. The assistant only acts on behalf of the user who is logged in.

## What the assistant can and cannot do
The assistant can answer questions about FitBook policies, check open slots, list your appointments, and book
or cancel on your behalf after you confirm. It cannot take payments, give medical advice, or change a
trainer's availability.
